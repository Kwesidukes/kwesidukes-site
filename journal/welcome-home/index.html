Welcome Home.

A night of basketball, four strangers from Bunbury, and two words that stayed with me.

By Kwesi Dukes · Published 26 November 2026

Everyone who knows me for real knows I love basketball. I love the game itself, but I also love watching someone learn it. Those beginning stages, when things start making sense and confidence begins to grow, mean something to me. Basketball has always been something I can connect with, wherever I am.

Yas and I recently went to RAC Arena here in Perth to watch the Australian national team play. I expected to have a good time. What I didn’t expect was how much I would feel the energy and pride around me. I’m American, but sitting there cheering alongside everyone, I felt connected to the people around me. I was cheering for Australia and feeling every bit of it.

To our right sat four guys from Bunbury. We started chatting, and they asked where I was from and how I was liking Australia. As the game went on, you would have thought we had all come there together. Between the basketball, the conversation, and them buying us more drinks than I can remember, we were having a time. A shared love of the game gave us somewhere to start, and the rest came easily.

Australia got the win. But something those guys said as we were leaving stayed with me longer than anything that happened on the court.

“Welcome home.”

Two words. They probably had no idea how much they meant to me.

I’ve been busy finding my footing here, working, studying, building my business, and figuring out life in a new country with my wife. Sometimes I’m so focused on what needs to happen next that I don’t stop to take in what’s happening now. That night, four guys we had only just met made me feel welcome in a way I’ll remember.

I came to Australia to build a life with Yas. Somewhere between tipoff and that goodbye, I felt a little more at home.
